About Paavai Innovation Forum

Paavai Innovation Forum is a dedicated platform that promotes innovation, research culture, and entrepreneurship among students and faculty.

It bridges academic knowledge with real-world applications through interdisciplinary collaboration and industry engagement.

About InnoSphere

InnoSphere is an interdisciplinary innovation club under Paavai Innovation Forum that nurtures creative thinking, problem solving, and research excellence.

The club empowers students to transform ideas into impactful solutions through projects, events, and collaborative learning.
